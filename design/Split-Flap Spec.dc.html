01 · TILE ANATOMY

Physical honesty comes from five layers, drawn in order.

  1. 1Housing bed. Flat fill behind the grid. Gap between flaps is 0.11H across and 0.17H down; the taller gap holds the flap stack.gapX 0.11 · gapY 0.17
  2. 2Next flaps peeking. Two edge lines under each flap, the second at 55% opacity and narrower, so the stack reads as depth. Hinge pins sit in the side gaps at 0.5H.+0.010H · +0.030H
  3. 3Flap face. Width 0.68H, corner radius 0.05H. One vertical gradient with a hard stop at the split: top half faceHi to face, bottom half faceB to faceLo.w 0.68 · r 0.05
  4. 4Glyph. Filled text, pre-rendered in the atlas. The split line cuts through the middle of the capitals.cap 0.46 · base 0.73
  5. 5Hinge. Occlusion gradients 0.08H above and 0.06H below the split, a crease of 0.022H (never under 1 device pixel), a 0.010H lip highlight under it, and pin notches cut 0.035W into each side edge.crease 0.022 · lip 0.010

No reflections, glare or gloss. Tile height is rounded to an even number of device pixels so the hinge lands on a pixel boundary and the crease stays crisp.

02 · THE FOLD, A TO B

A vertical scale of each half, plus three flat overlays.

Fold angle θ runs from 0 to π. While θ is under π/2 the old top half scales toward the hinge by cos θ and darkens as it tilts away from an overhead light. Past π/2 the new bottom half grows down from the hinge by −cos θ and brightens slightly, because it faces up. Each falling half casts a flat shadow on the face it is about to cover.

03 · GLYPH

Capitals centred on the hinge. Rings and dots stay inside the flap.

Cap height 0.46H with the baseline at 0.73H puts the cap top at 0.27H, so the split falls at the exact middle of every capital. Font size is cap height divided by the face's cap ratio. The Å ring and Ä Ö dots top out near 0.10H (solid line), leaving a clear margin to the flap edge.

DM Mono 500 for Black and White. Already self-hosted in /fonts/. SIL Open Font License 1.1.capRatio 0.70 · size 0.657H
Schibsted Grotesk 700 for Solari, the heavier grotesk of an airport hall. Self-host a Latin subset with Å Ä Ö and °. SIL Open Font License 1.1.capRatio 0.72 · size 0.639H

Drum order, 70 positions: space, A to Z, Å Ä Ö, 0 to 9, . , : ; ! ? ' " - + / & % # @ ( ) = °, then the nine colour chips. Common accents fold to their base letter (É to E, Ü to U, Ø to Ö, Æ to Ä). Anything else becomes a blank flap. Build the atlas after document.fonts.ready, and again on any tile size change.

05 · BOARD THEMES

Board, frame and wall, shown at 16:9.

The letterbox is treated as the wall the board hangs on: a quiet radial gradient, lightest just above centre, with a static contact shadow under the frame painted once into the background layer. The board never exceeds 88% of the limiting axis, so there is always wall on every side.

07 · MOTION

Gravity, a small rebound, and a stagger that reads as machinery.

θ = π · t1.35: the flap leaves the catch slowly and accelerates, crossing edge on at 60% of the flip. After the final character lands it rebounds 0.13 rad and settles. Intermediate drum steps skip the rebound.

One tile at roughly eight times slower than Fast, looping, so the shading on each half can be judged by eye.

Performance budget for a Raspberry Pi 4: device pixel ratio capped at 2 (the Pi runs at 1), one requestAnimationFrame loop that stops when no cell is moving, redraw only animating cells from a cached background slice, and a glyph atlas keyed by theme, character and tile size. A full 6 × 22 page change peaks near 800 draw calls per frame, all blits and flat fills.
