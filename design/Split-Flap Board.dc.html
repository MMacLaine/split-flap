Split-Flap

A departure board for the screen you already have.

Split-Flap turns a spare monitor into a mechanical style message board. Pages rotate through your own messages, the time, departures from Storstockholms Lokaltrafik (SL), the weather and countdowns, each letter flipping into place like a railway board.

It is free and needs no account. Everything you set up stays in this browser, and a board link carries it to another screen.

Put it on a monitor

01

Raspberry Pi

  1. Install Raspberry Pi OS with desktop and connect the monitor.
  2. Compose your board on your phone, then open the board link on the Pi.
  3. Add ?kiosk=1 to the link and set Chromium to open it at start up.
02

Casting a tab

  1. Open the board in Chrome on a computer on the same network as the television.
  2. Choose Cast from the browser menu and pick the tab.
  3. Press Fullscreen. Keep the computer awake while it casts.
03

An old laptop

  1. Turn off sleep and the screen saver in the power settings.
  2. Open the board link and press F for fullscreen.
  3. Set quiet hours so the screen dims overnight.

Questions

Is it really free?
Yes. There are no accounts, no adverts and nothing to install.
Where is my board stored?
In this browser only. Clearing site data removes it, so export a copy if it matters to you.
How do I move a board to the wall screen?
Press Share and open the link, or scan the QR (Quick Response) code, on the other screen. The link holds the whole board.
Why did a character turn blank?
The flaps carry capital letters, Å Ä Ö, numbers and common punctuation. Anything else shows as a blank flap, just like a real board.
What happens when the internet drops?
Messages and the clock keep running. Departures and weather hold their last values and a small note in the corner says how old they are.
