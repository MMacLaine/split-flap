Drill downNew level slides in 24px from the right and fades, 220ms cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1). The old level is replaced, not stacked. Scroll resets to top.Flips to the page being opened, with the board's own transition.
BackSame, mirrored: 24px from the left. Escape and the back control do the same thing.Zone highlight clears on leaving the content level.
Zone highlightDiagram outline and list marker switch to the accent instantly on hover or focus.Cells outside the zone get the housing colour at 62%, an accent line 0.05H wide runs in the gaps around the zone. Drawn by the renderer, no CSS on the canvas. Instant, so it tracks the pointer.
Tile landsPicker is replaced by the channel header and options, rising 10px and fading in over 280ms.Only the zone's cells flip to the new content, inside the highlight, so the eye goes straight to where it landed.
ReorderThe dragged page takes the accent tint and moves live as the pointer crosses the next item. Arrow keys on the handle do the same, focus follows the page.No change.
Reduced motionDrawer animations drop to 1ms. The board keeps its settled crossfade.
