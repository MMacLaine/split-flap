Each move to a new level pushes a browser history entry, so the browser Back and the phone back gesture go up one level, and reload lands in the same place. A tab returns to where you last were in that section, and tapping the current tab goes to its top. Sheets (Add a board, Copy to, Import) and menus are not levels: Back or Escape closes them first. Board links (#b=) are unchanged and sit outside the #/ paths.
